# Worksheet · 學習單

Static practice: kids remember through reading, writing, matching and drawing on paper. Use it alone or with activities — together they make up **more than half** of the lesson. Often the better fit for younger groups and schools without devices. Use `templates/worksheet.md`.

## Design
- Ask the designer what each section should do — they often specify it (e.g. "section 1: riddles describing each word in Chinese, kids fill in the English from a word bank; section 2: label the same words on a picture/map"). Propose section ideas only if they haven't.
- Every item uses only words and ideas taught in the lesson (no 超綱).
- Title each section "Action N 第 N 大題" with a bilingual instruction line, left-aligned.
- End with something every child can succeed at (drawing, opinion).
- The number of sections follows the lesson — the template shows five, but fewer is fine.

Section ideas by level:

| Younger / Basic (grades 1–3) | Older / Advanced (grades 4–6) |
|---|---|
| Picture ↔ word matching, circle the right picture | Fill-in sentences with the core pattern |
| Riddles: Chinese description → English word from a word bank | Label a picture, map or diagram |
| Trace / write key words | Short reasoning: why / what happens if |
| Label a simple picture | Apply to a real case |
| Draw your favourite … | Open question with reasons |

### Open-format worksheets
For applying ideas to real cases (Advanced), an open format often works better than fill-in lines: a few boxes with headings, where kids may use keywords, bullet points or drawings.

## Versions
- **Student version 學生版** — zhuyin on all Chinese whenever the class includes grade 1–3 students (`zhuyin.md`)
- **Volunteer version 志工版／答案版** — answers, marking advice (tracing and drawing reward participation, not correctness), common mistakes and how to guide kids back. Clearly marked「志工用 — 請勿發給學生」.

## Layout
- Any class with grade 1–3 students: 16pt+ (zhuyin needs the size), line spacing ~2.0, mostly Chinese with English support.
- Grades 4–6 only: ~12pt, spacing 1.5, mostly English with Chinese support.
- White background, minimal, plenty of writing space, prints well on A4 in black and white.

After the worksheet, go over 1–2 items aloud.

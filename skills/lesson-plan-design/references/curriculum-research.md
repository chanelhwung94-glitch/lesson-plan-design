# Curriculum Research · 課綱與課本調查

Do this before outlining. The aim: know what students at each grade have **already learned at school** about this topic, in any subject (social studies, science, life skills…, not only English), and what they haven't.

## What to look up
The sources below are the **defaults for Taiwanese elementary schools**. If the learners are elsewhere, use their own curriculum or syllabus, textbooks and word lists or level framework instead — ask the designer which ones.

1. **National curriculum** for the relevant subject(s) — which grade introduces the topic (in Taiwan: 108 課綱 by 領域 and 學習階段).
2. **Main textbook series by grade and unit** — in Taiwan, 康軒、翰林、南一. Note the grade/semester and unit where the topic appears.
3. **English word lists** for vocabulary range — e.g. the MOE elementary 1000 words and 1200 thematic words, GEPT Kids. These check words, not concepts.
4. Anything the designer adds (their school's textbook, their own list).

Use web search for public curriculum documents, publishers' unit lists and teaching handouts. Cite each source. If you can't confirm something, write 待核對 — don't guess.

The word lists cover all of elementary school and aren't split by grade, so a word being on a list doesn't mean a grade-3 class has learned it — judge grade level from the curriculum and textbook units. Use textbooks only to see what is taught when; never copy their text, pictures or exercises.

## What the research decides
- **Is the topic worth a lesson?** Content school doesn't teach (or teaches later) is where the lesson adds most.
- **How Advanced is built and how far Basic is simplified.** Advanced is designed first; Basic is simplified from it. The research tells you how far:
  - Already taught at school → don't re-teach; review briefly and extend (sentences, relationships, applying it). Usually the Advanced case.
  - Not yet taught → that level starts from zero: recognise and name. Often where Basic lands.
- **What concepts are safe for mixed-grade groups** — only what the youngest grade in the group has learned.
- **Which items are above level** — concepts or words beyond what the grade has learned. They can still be used when the lesson needs them, but mark them ⚠️ 超出程度 and plan extra support and practice.
